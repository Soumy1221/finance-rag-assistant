# Invoice Dispute Policy (Synthetic Demo)

Customers should raise invoice disputes within 30 calendar days of the invoice date.
A dispute request must include the invoice number, disputed line item, reason, and
supporting evidence where available. Finance Operations acknowledges a complete dispute
within 3 business days and aims to provide an outcome within 10 business days.
Undisputed portions remain payable by the original due date. Raising a dispute does not
automatically suspend payment of the full invoice.
